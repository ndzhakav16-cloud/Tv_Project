# TV_Project
Supporting the CEO’s Objective to Grow the Subscription Base

# Introduction

This project focuses on analyzing customer profiles and viewing patterns to provide insights that will help grow BrightTV’s subscription base. To remain competitive in the market, the company must understand its customers better and use data-driven strategies to drive growth. By leveraging customer behavior and engagement data, BrightTV can optimize its product offerings and strengthen retention strategies.

# Problem Statement

The CEO of BrightTV has set a key objective for this financial year: to increase the company’s subscription base. However, the business currently lacks detailed insights into critical areas such as:
* Which customer segments are most engaged
* Which content genres or shows attract the most viewers
* How demographics (age, gender, location) influence viewing behavior
* Retention challenges and opportunities for cross-selling or upselling
NB: Without these insights, the CVM (Customer Value Management) team may struggle to design effective customer acquisition and retention strategies.

# Aim

The aim of this project is to analyze BrightTV’s customer and transaction data to uncover patterns, trends, and behaviors that can support the growth of its subscription base.

# Objectives

* Identify which TV channels and content types are most popular among different customer groups, categorized by location and usage behavior.
* Analyze overall customer engagement levels and identify high-value subscribers who contribute most to viewership and revenue.
* Highlight opportunities for targeted marketing, personalized recommendations, and enhanced retention strategies.

# Summary of Report

The report highlights the most profitable customer segments, the most popular shows and genres, and the behavioral patterns that influence subscription decisions. Insights from this analysis will enable the CVM team to design effective strategies to attract new subscribers, retain existing ones, and ultimately support the CEO’s goal of growing BrightTV’s subscription base for the current financial year.
